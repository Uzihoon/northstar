# General Overview & Tips

Seoul is a modern, bustling city that blends ancient traditions with cutting-edge modernity. It is considered a safe city, with violent crime and petty crimes being rare. The city is a global hub for culture, art, and technology, and is known for its vibrant gastronomy scene, K-Pop, and K-Beauty.

**Best Time to Visit:**
*   **Spring (March to May):** Temperatures are pleasantly warm, and the city hosts famous festivals like the Lotus Lantern Festival. April is noted for beautiful cherry blossoms.
*   **Autumn (September to November):** The fall foliage is beautiful, with trees displaying vibrant red, orange, and yellow colors.
*   **Winter:** Daytime temperatures are warm, but evenings and mornings can be chilly.

**Key Experiences:**
*   **Culture & History:** Exploring ancient palaces, traditional villages (like Bukchon Hanok Village and Ikseon-Dong), and historic sites.
*   **Modern Life:** Experiencing trendy areas, contemporary architecture, and art galleries.
*   **Gastronomy:** Seoul is a foodie's paradise with a mix of street food markets and fine dining.

**Practical Information:**
*   **Transportation:** The public transportation system (subway, bus, and tram) is efficient and easy to use. The T-money or WOWPASS card is recommended for payment on public transport and shopping. Google Maps does not provide walking or driving directions in Korea; Naver Map is recommended for this purpose. Kakao is the popular ride-share app, as Uber's effectiveness can be limited.
*   **Connectivity:** Downloading Google Translate is recommended for real-time translation of signs and menus.
*   **Safety:** Staying alert and using well-lit streets at night is advised.
