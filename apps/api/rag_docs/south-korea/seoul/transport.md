# Getting Around Seoul

Seoul has an efficient public transportation system, making it easy to navigate the city.

*   **Payment:** A T-money or WOWPASS card is recommended for transportation and shopping. These cards can be topped up at convenience stores.
*   **Subway/Bus:** The subway system is extensive, with lines operating from early morning until late at night. Buses are also well-developed and color-coded by area.
*   **Taxis:** Taxis are a cost-efficient option.
*   **Ride-Share:** Kakao is the most popular ride-share app in South Korea; Uber's effectiveness can be limited.
*   **Bicycles:** Seoul Public Bike (Ttareungi) is an unmanned bike rental system available throughout the city.
*   **Airport Transfer:** Incheon International Airport is the main port of arrival. Buses are a recommended way to get into the city center.
*   **Navigation Apps:** While Google Maps works for public transport, Naver Map is recommended for detailed walking or driving directions, as Google Maps does not provide these in Korea.
