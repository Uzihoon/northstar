# Top Attractions and Activities

Seoul offers a mix of historical, cultural, and modern attractions:

**Historical & Cultural Sites:**
*   **Palaces:** Gyeongbokgung Palace is highlighted as an iconic attraction. Other palaces are also worth exploring.
*   **Traditional Villages:** Bukchon Hanok Village and Ikseon-Dong Hanok Village offer insight into traditional Korean culture.
*   **Markets:** Myeongdong Market (known for street food), Gwangjang Market (for food and textiles), and Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market.
*   **Historical Walls:** Hanyangdoseong City Walls offer opportunities for walking trails.
*   **Museums:** The National Museum of Korea is home to ancient artifacts.

**Modern & Leisure Activities:**
*   **Parks & Rivers:** Cheonggyecheon Stream is a long public recreation space perfect for walking. Seoul Forest is a large area incorporating the Han River and Seongsu-dong.
*   **Shopping & Beauty:** Myeongdong is a major shopping destination.
