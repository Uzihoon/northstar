# Must-Try Korean Cuisine & Dining

Seoul offers a wide variety of dining experiences, from street food to fine dining. Must-try dishes include:

*   **Korean BBQ:** Cooking your own meat on a hot plate with rice, vegetables, and kimchi.
*   **Korean Fried Chicken:** Popular with flavors like Gochujang, known for being super crispy.
*   **Mandu:** Korean dumplings, often filled with pork, vegetables, shrimp, or kimchi.
*   **Tteokbokki:** Chewy rice cakes cooked in a spicy red sauce.
*   **Bibimbap:** A rice bowl topped with sautéed and seasoned vegetables, raw egg yolk, and thin slices of beef.
*   **Street Food:** Popular snacks include Bindaetteok (mung bean pancake), Odeng or Eomuk (fishcakes on a stick), and Hotteok (pancake with cinnamon or brown sugar filling).
*   **Other Dishes:** Bulgogi (thin slices of marinated beef or pork), Gimbap (seaweed filled with rice and pickled veggies), and Kal-guksu (handmade, knife-cut noodles).

**Dining Areas:**
*   **Myeongdong:** A popular area for street food markets at night.
*   **Gwangjang Market:** Famous for street food, including Bindaetteok and noodles.
*   **Noryangjin Fisheries Wholesale Market:** A place to observe fresh seafood or purchase and have it cooked.
*   **Restaurants:** Michelin-rated restaurants are available for fine dining experiences.
