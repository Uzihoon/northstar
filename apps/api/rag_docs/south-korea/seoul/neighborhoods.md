# Key Neighborhoods to Explore

Seoul offers several distinct districts, each with its own unique character:

*   **Jongno:** Located in the heart of the city, this area is central and walkable. It is near the Han River, making it lovely for walks. It features many bars and restaurants and is close to iconic buildings.
*   **Myeongdong:** Known as a major shopping and street food destination.
*   **Other Key Areas:** Include areas like Gangnam (high-end shopping/beauty), Hongdae (younger, artsy vibe), and Itaewon (nightlife/restaurants).
