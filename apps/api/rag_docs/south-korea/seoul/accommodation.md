# Accommodation Options

Accommodation options range from budget-friendly hostels to high-end luxury hotels. Booking in advance is highly recommended as the city is very busy.

**Budget/Hostel Options:**
*   **Hostels:** Options are available in central areas like Itaewon and Myeongdong, suitable for budget travelers and meeting other travelers.
*   **Mid-Range Hotels:** Hotels near major metro access (e.g., Seoul Station area) offer reliability and easy transport.
*   **High-End/Luxury Hotels:** Luxury options are available in central areas like Gwanghwamun and Myeongdong, offering premium service and central convenience.
