# Kyoto Restaurants

Kyoto has many food options, including traditional Japanese meals, casual restaurants, cafes, bakeries, tofu cuisine, Buddhist vegetarian cuisine, ramen shops, curry, and modern plant-forward restaurants.

## Vegetarian-Friendly Planning

Vegetarian travelers should not assume every Japanese restaurant can easily adapt dishes, because dashi, fish stock, bonito flakes, and hidden meat-based broths are common. It is better to choose vegetarian-friendly restaurants intentionally.

Good vegetarian-friendly categories:
- Shojin ryori
- Tofu restaurants
- Vegetable-focused set meals
- Vegan ramen
- Curry restaurants with vegetarian options
- Modern cafes with plant-based dishes

## Meal Planning Notes

For relaxed travel, avoid making every meal a destination. Pick one important meal per day and keep the other meal flexible near the current neighborhood.

If the itinerary includes popular areas like Higashiyama, Gion, or Arashiyama, plan lunch slightly early or slightly late to avoid peak crowds.

## Useful Food Preferences

Northstar should preserve these preferences when planning:
- Vegetarian food
- Quiet atmosphere
- Cafe breaks
- Local but not stressful
- Flexible meal timing
