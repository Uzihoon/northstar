# Kyoto Transport

Kyoto travel often involves a mix of trains, buses, walking, and taxis. Travel time can be meaningful, so itineraries should include transport blocks instead of pretending locations are instantly connected.

## General Transport Notes

Kyoto buses can be useful but may be crowded or slow during peak travel times. Trains are often more predictable when available. Walking is part of the Kyoto experience, but walking time should be planned realistically.

## Planning Rules

- Group sights by neighborhood.
- Avoid crossing the city more than necessary.
- Add transport time between neighborhoods.
- Include buffer time before meals or reservations.
- Use taxis selectively when they reduce stress.

## Approximate Timing

These are rough planning estimates, not exact routing results.

- Kyoto Station to Higashiyama: 20 to 35 minutes
- Kyoto Station to Arashiyama: 25 to 45 minutes
- Downtown Kyoto to Gion: 10 to 20 minutes
- Gion to Philosopher's Path area: 20 to 35 minutes
- Downtown Kyoto to Fushimi Inari: 20 to 35 minutes

## Itinerary Notes

For a relaxed two-day itinerary, each day should focus on one or two main areas. Transport blocks should include duration estimates and should not be hidden inside attraction descriptions.
